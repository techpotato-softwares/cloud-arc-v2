# Buying and delivery FAQ

## What do I receive?

A licensed source artifact for the selected plan, its frozen dependency lock, generated contracts, infrastructure code, tests, and a setup guide. Delivery starts only after verified payment.

## Which payment provider is used?

India billing uses Razorpay in rupees. International billing uses Stripe in US dollars.

## Does my application depend on ForgeArc servers?

No. You deploy the source into infrastructure you control. Commerce and licensed delivery are the only ForgeArc-hosted steps.

## Which prices are authoritative?

The checkout catalog is authoritative. Documentation does not duplicate price constants.

## What is available today?

ForgeArc Node, ForgeArc Python, their bundle, and ForgeArc AI Starter AWS. GCP and plans depending on GCP remain visibly marked as roadmap and cannot be checked out.

## Are testimonials fabricated?

No. Until approved customer references exist, ForgeArc publishes verifiable product evidence, source previews, acceptance tests, and delivery guarantees instead.
